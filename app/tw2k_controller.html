A disabled motor is free: it can be turned by hand and J2/J3 can drop under gravity. Support the arm first, and re-home after re-enabling if a joint was moved.

Zero and limits

If a joint moves less (or more) than the app shows: jog it, note the angle change in the app, measure the real change, then press fix from measurement.

To find a joint's real travel: type a wider limit, jog slowly to the physical end, then press max here or min here. zero here makes the current position 0° for that joint and is re-applied after every homing. All of this is saved on the Teensy.

Homing and endstops · not homed

If a joint homes the wrong way: press Stop homing, jog it with − or + to find which button moves it toward its switch, then set toward to that button and home again. The setting is saved on the Teensy.

J6 has no switch, so it counts its power-up position as zero. Line J6 up by eye before homing.

G-code drawing

  1. Hold the pen at the tool angle you like (flange facing +X with the pen pointing down is fine). Enter the pen offset below.
  2. Jog the pen tip onto the paper where the drawing should be (or just above it), then press Set drawing origin here.
  3. Load or generate G-code, press Check path, then Run. Test pen down/up is optional.
